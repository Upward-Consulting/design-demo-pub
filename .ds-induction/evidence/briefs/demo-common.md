## What to build

The root carries `data-demo="<L>"`.

**Pages (same keys in every demo):** `home`, `products`, `corrosion`, `contact`. All are `page` mode (utility). **Home takes one story opener** (owner ruling): its first chapter is a `StoryChapter` on the kit, `model="lock"`, `hold={1}` (one screen held, no more), the **h1 and the primary action ("Talk to an expert" and the phone number) visible and readable at p = 0**, driven by GSAP (`useChapterProgress` → `tl.progress(p)`, paused timeline; the pattern in the stack sheet). It rests (finished composition, nothing moving) under reduced motion and on the phone plan, and when its words do not fit a screen at 200% text. Everything after the opener is ordinary utility page content with the motion layer below.

## Rules in force

- **Taste profile:** `adopted: all`, with the owner's instruction "adopt necessary ones and ones that don't go against our initiative [a stunning refreshed design]". So: the gate runs and every FAIL is fixed. If your dealt direction truly needs a device the house rules ban (a hairline, a mono label, a gradient, an accent word, a resting shadow, a tinted-icon card, a particular `--allow` rule id), do not hide it: use it sparingly, name the rule id, and report it under `allowances` with one sentence on why the direction needs it. The lead rules on each allowance for the owner. **Prefer the house-rule alternative** (space, a tone step, a filled shape, a thick bar). Never `taste-skill-ignore`.
- **Motion** (CSS, GSAP on Home only): `motion-standards.md` § 3 numbers for utility pages: hover 200 ms; a label roll or fill 300–400 ms; popover 240 ms; accordion 320–450 ms; entrances 600–800 ms with 12–24 px travel (or a line mask), stagger 50–90 ms, triggered at 85–90% of the viewport; transform and opacity only; no overshoot or elastic easing; the direction's signature move (named on your sheet's Motion row) and **at least eight elements moving** on every page (the gate's floor); feedback on every control; one moving picture per page where an honest one exists (a slow crop or reveal of a real photograph counts; there is no video). **`prefers-reduced-motion`**: the finished composition, nothing animating (colour-only transitions of 200 ms or less allowed). No scroll hijack, no custom cursor, no smoothing library, no sound.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 AA. One `h1` per page, landmarks, text contrast measured (ink on every field, text on every picture), body text 16 px or more and nothing under 13 px, visible focus, targets 44 px on touch, pinch-zoom is never disabled (you do not touch the viewport tag), nothing depends on hover. Phone is its own composition, not the desktop squeezed.
- **No sideways scroll** at 1440, 820 and 390 (and 320 for the story check). The primary action is visible on the first screen of every page.
- **Dependencies:** none added. Icons are inline SVG you write. No other libraries.
- **Forbidden:** writing outside your lane (token file, `index.css`, `index.html`, `manifest.ts`, `system.ts`, `demo-copy.ts`, `pictures.ts`, the story kit, the workbench, another demo's folder, `package.json`), opening `?view=demos` or another demo's folder, `draw.mjs` in any form, starting or stopping a dev server, any git or install command, `--fix`, any port but yours.

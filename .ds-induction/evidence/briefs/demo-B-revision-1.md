## C. Make it stunning (this is the real job)

The jurors' verdict: the first screen is strong; after it the poster idea stops, the colour fields are mostly empty ground with 13–16 px text and small pictures in corners, the Products page reads as a catalogue kit, and the phone is the desktop squeezed. Fix it by design, not by decoration:

1. **Statement fields (the five jobs).** Each job is a full-bleed colour poster panel with one blunt statement at 7–9vw, its picture (a cut-out or a tilted/cropped photograph) at least 35–45% of the field width and breaking the field edge, the body at 20 px or more, and the "Materials" line as bold slips instead of 13 px grey text. No panel is more than about 70% empty. Alternate alignment so the page zig-zags. Respect each picture's `maxW`.
2. **The four-step accordion:** the open step must be filled (a big step word at poster size, the body at 20 px or more, a picture or a slip row), not a 400 px empty orange field with a clipped vertical word.
3. **Proof tiles:** the named sites are big type on flat tiles, content-height (no 150 px of empty tile), 22–28 px names.
4. **Home must show the five branches** (city, address from the deck, with the main line, email and "Talk to an expert" cells), under a heading from the deck. The first build's "Five branches" section showed none.
5. **Carry the poster language onto the inner pages.** Products: the six job filters as large flat colour buttons (one field per job, keyboard and `aria-pressed` as before), the families as poster panels with the family name huge, the lines as bold slips, a cut-out or photo per family where the pool has one (families with no picture get a flat colour field with the name, not an empty slot). 6. Keep control: a strict grid, two or three colours at a time, one button shape, one corner radius, exact alignment. Loud, not messy. Pink and cyan stay sparing; the yellow and black voice dominates. Contrast of every ink on every field measured.
7. Every page needs motion that serves the poster idea (masked headline, a field that slides in, a cut-out that tilts in) within `motion-standards.md` § 3 and the reduced-motion rule, and at least eight moving elements per page.

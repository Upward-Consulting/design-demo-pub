# Inventory: what only Bellis has

From the dossier and the asset review. Each line is also a picture search or a device candidate.

1. **The mark is a pipe elbow.** The logo is a black stem with a blue quarter-arc. A quarter-arc is the profile of a pipe bend, and lobster-back bends are on Bellis's own fabrication list.
2. **Two inks.** Black and `#0065A3`, as printed in the logo. The brand is already a two-colour print job.
3. **Since 1981.** Corrosion-under-insulation work and the supplier network both date from 1981 (the site's other claims of "30" and "35 years" are inconsistent, so the copy says only "since 1981" and the open question is logged).
4. **The Quick Look index.** About 90 named product lines: Foamglas, Pyrogel XTE / XTF / HPS, Cryogel Z, Thermobreak, Firemaster 550, Versi-Foam, Armaflex, Interam, Rockwool. A catalogue with real names.
5. **Five branches, one number.** Coopers Plains (Brisbane head office), Callemondah (Gladstone), Epping (Melbourne), Woolner (Darwin), Henderson (Perth), each with its street address; 1300 740 564 reaches the company. Two workshops, Brisbane and Gladstone, with CNC sheet-metal and plasma cutting.
6. **Five jobs for a layer of insulation.** Hold temperature, keep people from hot surfaces, quieten plant, hold fire, stop corrosion under insulation. The dossier says to navigate by problem as well as by industry.
7. **The enemy: CUI.** Corrosion under insulation: moisture trapped against steel. The client's own picture of a rusted flange and a rusted pipe. The service: remove and inspect, paint, reinstate with a vapour barrier or closed-cell system.
8. **Metal made to measure.** A polished stainless cladding job the size of a road tanker, lobster-back bends, valve and flange boxes, red removable covers with window labels, a fabric expansion joint stencilled "BELLIS AUSTRALIA".
9. **Hi-vis crews on plant roofs.** The LNG carrier, the roof of a plant under scaffold, the Coopers Plains depot with the Bellis sign: the real picture supply.
10. **The sites.** Power stations (Gladstone, Tarong, Swanbank, Stanwell, Channel Island, Mica Creek); refineries and smelters (Caltex Lytton, Yarwun, Boyne, Olympic Dam, Ranger). Named on the client's own site, so text-only proof the client has already published.
11. **The shutdown window.** Planned maintenance and breakdown work on tight windows: the constraint the business lives with, and the reason supply reliability is the selling point.
12. **The printed things of its world.** An estimate that runs from feasibility to actual cost, a datasheet, a coil label, a stainless-banding barcode, a stencilled "BELLIS AUSTRALIA" on a joint.

## Products (`products`, utility page)

1. About 90 lines in 11 families; find yours by problem (hot, cold, acoustic, fire, corrosion, access) or by name. Proof: the catalogue itself, de-duplicated, with real names (list).
2. Every family ends in "Enquire now" (cta), because the site sells by quote and shows no prices.

## Corrosion control (`corrosion`, utility page: service detail)

1. Corrosion under insulation is a major maintenance and shutdown cost. Proof: the client's rusted-flange picture (hero).
2. Bellis has dealt with it since 1981 and does the whole job. Proof: the three steps (remove and inspect, paint, reinstate with vapour barrier or closed-cell) (how-it-works).
3. The products that prevent it. Proof: anticorrosion wrap, mastics, vapour barriers (list).
4. Talk to an expert (cta).

2. Or send the details once and Bellis replies. Proof: a form with validation and error states: name, company, email, phone, branch, what you need (contact).
